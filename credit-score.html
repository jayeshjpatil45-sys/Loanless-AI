Score Guide

Understanding Credit Scores

Excellent (750–900)

You're a low-risk borrower. Enjoy the best interest rates, highest credit limits, and premium loan offers from all financial institutions.

Good (650–749)

You have a fair credit profile. Most lenders will approve your applications with competitive rates. Minor improvements can unlock premium benefits.

Poor (300–649)

Your credit needs attention. Focus on timely repayments, reducing outstanding debts, and improving credit utilization to boost your score.
