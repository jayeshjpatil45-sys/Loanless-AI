Quick Tips

Essential Financial Tips

1

Maintain a Credit Score Above 750

A score above 750 unlocks the best interest rates and premium loan offers. Pay bills on time, keep credit utilization below 30%, and avoid multiple loan applications.

2

Follow the 50/30/20 Budget Rule

Allocate 50% of income to needs, 30% to wants, and 20% to savings & debt repayment. This ensures sustainable financial health and loan repayment capacity.

3

Keep EMI-to-Income Ratio Below 40%

Lenders prefer borrowers whose total EMI obligations don't exceed 40% of monthly income. This improves your loan approval chances significantly.

4

Build an Emergency Fund First

Maintain 6-12 months of expenses in liquid savings before taking loans. This protects you from defaulting during unexpected financial disruptions.

5

Compare Loan Offers Thoroughly

Interest rates vary by 1-3% across lenders. A 1% difference on a ₹50L home loan over 20 years can save over ₹7 lakhs. Always compare before committing.

6

Make Prepayments When Possible

Even small prepayments significantly reduce interest burden over the loan tenure. Prioritize prepaying high-interest loans first for maximum savings.
